Farewell from USTCAF Internet Service

Dear Donors,

Since 2016, USTCAF Internet Service has been honored to serve the community with your generous support. Today, the time has come for us to say goodbye.

The service is expected to be shut down by the end of May, and we kindly ask you to make necessary arrangements in advance.

All related software has been fully open-sourced on GitHub. If you wish to continue using it, you are welcome to deploy it on your own:

  • https://github.com/ustclug/lug-vpn-web/tree/ustcaf (Anyconnect version)
  • https://github.com/ustclug/lug-vpn-web/tree/ustcaf-wg (WireGuard version)

Thank you for your trust and continued support over the years.

Sincerely,
USTCAF Internet Service Team
